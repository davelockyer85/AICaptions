PART 1: PRIVACY POLICY

1. Introduction

AICaptions ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of all live audio, video, and text data processed through our real-time transcription and captioning platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your information when you access or use the AICaptions web application, APIs, or event services.

This policy complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable global data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Operational Data

  • User Information: Name, email address, billing details, company/organization name, and contact details provided upon account registration.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device specs, and session log files collected automatically for system performance and security.

B. Audio & Media Data (Service Inputs)

  • Live Audio Streams & Files: Real-time microphone audio feeds, network streams (e.g., NDI/SRT/RTMP), or uploaded media files submitted for automated captioning.
  • Metadata: Technical parameters of input streams (sample rate, stream duration, latency stats).

C. Generated Captions & Outputs

  • Transcripts & Caption Files: Text outputs, SRT/VTT files, and live event logs produced during processing.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process data strictly for operational, security, and service delivery purposes:

  • Service Provision: To transcribe, convert, and render live audio into real-time captions.
  • Account Management: To manage subscriptions, process payments, and provide customer support.
  • System Health: To monitor API uptime, latency, error rates, and security threats.

CRITICAL COMMITMENT: Zero AI Training on Live Client Audio

We DO NOT use customer live audio, video, or generated captions to train, fine-tune, or improve public or proprietary AI models.

Audio streams are processed in-memory (RAM) in real time and are purged immediately after processing, unless explicitly stored or logged by you via account settings.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Live Streamed Audio: Transient; processed immediately in RAM and deleted in real-time. Never written to permanent disk storage.
  • Generated Transcripts: Retained in your account dashboard according to your configured retention settings. You may manually delete transcripts or set automated purge schedules at any time.
  • Account Information: Retained as long as your account remains active or as required by law for financial accounting (e.g., tax receipts).

5. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

AICaptions utilizes enterprise-grade infrastructure providers to deliver high-availability, low-latency live transcription.

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Secure cloud data centers (e.g., AWS / Google Cloud) situated in region-locked compliance centers.
  • Payment Processing: Transactions are handled via PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe). AICaptions does not store raw credit card credentials.
  • Sub-processors: Any sub-processor used for AI speech inference operates under strict contractual obligations guaranteeing zero model training and strict data isolation.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS / WSS).
  • Encryption at rest (AES-256) for stored user transcripts and system configurations.
  • Strict role-based access controls (RBAC) and network isolation.

7. User Rights & Data Control

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal information.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your account and associated data ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Export your account data and transcript histories.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@aicaptions.com.

8. Contact Us

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy:

  • Email: privacy@aicaptions.com
  • Location: Perth, Western Australia, Australia
